Methodology

Our Proven 6-Step Work Process

From concept ideation to final key delivery, our structured workflow guarantees complete transparency and seamless execution.

Step 01

Initial Consultation

We meet to understand your goals, budget parameters, site condition, and architectural aspirations.

Step 02

Planning & Architectural Design

Drafting 2D blueprints, 3D photorealistic renderings, spatial layouts, and obtaining municipal approvals.

Step 03

Cost Estimation & Contracting

Providing comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material specifications, fixed timeline, and transparent contract.

Step 04

Ground Execution & Construction

Active site mobilization, foundation pouring, structural framing, MEP installation, and architectural finishes.

Step 05

Quality & Safety Inspection

Rigorous multi-point testing for structural integrity, electrical safety, plumbing pressure, and aesthetic detailing.

Step 06

Project Handover & Warranty

Final walk-through, key handover, warranty documentation, and ongoing structural maintenance support.
